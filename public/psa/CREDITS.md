# PSA demo card images — credits & licenses

Twelve vintage trading-card scans used as placeholder art for the PSA-inspired
graded-card collection UI. **Every image below is public domain or CC0** — free
for any use, commercial included, no attribution legally required. Credits are
recorded here anyway as good practice.

All files were fetched from Wikimedia Commons (which re-hosts scans from the
Library of Congress and the Metropolitan Museum of Art) at ~960px wide via the
Commons thumbnail API. `psacard.com` itself is Cloudflare-blocked and was not used.

Fetched 2026-07-31.

wider than tobacco cards. Swap it if a uniform grid matters more than having Ruth.
